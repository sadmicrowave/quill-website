Quill: Say It. With Quill. Talk, and each value lands in the right column of your spreadsheet.

Dictate

Just talk.

Say “black t-shirt, large, good condition, thirty five dollars.” Each word you say is understood and placed in its column as you say it, and you can see it happen before anything is written.

Say “next row” and the row is saved into your spreadsheet. No typing, no clicking between cells.

Term Helper

A word it doesn't know? Answer by voice.

New values wait in the Term Helper instead of going into the wrong cell. Say “color… yes” to add one, “same as L” to teach a synonym, or “ignore.” Several answers in one breath work too.

While you answer, Quill listens only for the words an answer is made of, so a TV or a nearby conversation can't answer for you.

It learns

It learns your words, and your habits.

Say “sold means sale date” once and Quill knows. Trim a stray word off a term (“kinda cream” to “cream”) and it skips that word from then on. Everything it has learned is listed in Settings, where you can edit or forget any of it.

Mini view

Small when you need it.

Shrink Quill to a slim strip that floats beside your spreadsheet: Start or Stop, the Term Helper count and Next Row, nothing else. Drag it taller whenever you want to see the words it heard and the row so far.

Works with

The spreadsheet you already have.

Excel, Numbers and LibreOffice Calc, with rows appearing live in the open document as you speak. Quill reads your own headings and drop-down lists, so there is nothing to set up, and nothing to learn about templates.

  • Microsoft Excel
  • Apple Numbers
  • LibreOffice Calc
  • .xlsx and .csv files

Private

Your voice stays yours.

Speech recognition runs on your computer. Nothing you say is sent anywhere, and Quill works without an internet connection. The only thing it ever asks the internet is whether a newer version exists.
