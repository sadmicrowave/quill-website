Mac

Works with Numbers, Excel and LibreOffice. Rows appear live in the open document.

Download for Mac

For Macs with an Apple chip (M1 or newer). Intel Mac? Download the Intel version. (Apple menu > About This Mac shows which one you have.)

  1. Open the download and drag Quill to your Applications folder.
  2. Open Quill. The first time, macOS says it cannot check Quill for malicious software, because it is not signed with a paid Apple certificate. Choose Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and press Open Anyway. (On older macOS, right-click Quill and choose Open.) You only do this once.
  3. Allow the microphone, and allow Quill to control your spreadsheet program when it asks.
  4. Open a spreadsheet, press Start Listening, and talk.

Windows

Works with Excel and LibreOffice, and with .xlsx and .csv files.

Download for Windows
  1. Unzip the download and open Quill.exe. Windows may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” box, because Quill is not signed with a paid certificate: choose More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once.
  2. Allow the microphone when asked.
  3. Open a spreadsheet, press Start Listening, and talk.

Good to know

Private
Speech recognition runs on your computer. Nothing you say is sent anywhere.
Up to date
Quill checks for a newer version when it starts, and tells you in Settings > About.
